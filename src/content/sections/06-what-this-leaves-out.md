---

This simulator keeps a few things simple on purpose, and it is worth naming them. Real conversion rates drift as trends, seasons, and player tastes shift, while this race mostly assumes a fixed truth unless you turn drift on yourself. Real campaigns interact with fatigue, saturation, and each other in ways three independent arms do not capture. And there is a line worth sitting with: optimizing what converts best is not automatically the same as optimizing what is good for players, and a bandit pointed at the wrong metric will cheerfully, efficiently find the most effective way to make that mistake. None of that makes the underlying idea less useful — it just means the honest use of a bandit starts with picking a metric worth being efficient about.
