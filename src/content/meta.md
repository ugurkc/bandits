---
eyebrow: An interactive essay on decisions under uncertainty
title: Bandit
---

Every week a studio decides where to put its marketing budget, which offer to test, which matchmaking tweak to ship — with only noisy, partial evidence about what actually works, and real money riding on the guess. This is the k-armed bandit problem, and the two acts below let you feel it before any algorithm explains itself: run a handful of ad campaigns by hand and watch how little five noisy days actually tells you, then hand the decision to a strategy and watch it out-learn you by making the same trade-off — explore versus exploit — on purpose, systematically, thousands of times over.
